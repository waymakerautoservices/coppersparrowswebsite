Refund Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

Business Entity: Copper Sparrows Attic Restoration LLC

At Copper Sparrows Attic Restoration LLC, we are committed to transparent, fair business practices. Because our services involve customized field labor, specialized safety logistics, and material staging, our refund guidelines are structured as follows:

1. Consultations, Remote Estimates & On-Site Inspections

Initial remote estimates and project scope reviews requested through our website forms or Calendly are free of charge. If a comprehensive on-site diagnostic inspection is performed under a separate paid inspection agreement, that inspection fee is strictly non-refundable once the physical evaluation, testing, or attic inspection has been conducted.

2. Project Deposits, Square Transactions & Cancellation Rules

For scheduled on-site projects requiring a material or project deposit processed securely via third-party payment gateways such as Square:

  • Standard 3-Day Cancellation Window & Material Hold: In compliance with Connecticut home improvement laws, clients have the right to cancel this transaction within three (3) business days of signing. To protect both parties, no material purchases, custom orders, or on-site work will be initiated until this 3-day cancellation window has fully expired.
  • Standard Cancellations (Within 3 Days): If a client exercises their legal right to cancel within the standard 3-day window (and no emergency waiver was executed), any deposit paid will be fully refunded, minus any non-recoverable third-party payment processing fees (e.g., Square transaction fees) retained by the payment processor.
  • Standard Cancellations (After 3 Days): For cancellations made after the statutory window has passed but at least 48 hours prior to the scheduled project commencement date, deposits are refundable minus processing fees and any non-refundable costs for specialized materials already ordered. Cancellations made less than 48 hours prior to the start time will forfeit the deposit.
  • Emergency Waiver Exception & Strict No-Refund Rule: If a client certifies a bona fide immediate property or health crisis by signing a formal, handwritten Emergency Work Waiver, the 3-day statutory cooling-off period is waived. The client acknowledges and agrees that they are solely responsible for verifying the legitimacy of their emergency condition; Copper Sparrows Attic Restoration LLC relies on this written certification in good faith and bears no liability if the circumstance fails to qualify legally. Under emergency conditions, once material procurement, staging, or on-site labor has commenced, there are no refunds whatsoever for deposits, payments, or work in progress.

3. Non-Refundable Services & Consumable Labor

Once on-site labor has officially commenced—including bio-hazard cleanout, insulation extraction, structural prep, or physical exclusion work—all payments, deposits, and milestone draws for work completed or underway are strictly non-refundable due to the consumable nature of remediation labor and specialized materials.

4. Chargebacks & Payment Disputes

Initiating a chargeback or payment dispute through Square, a credit card issuer, or a bank for services rendered, valid deposits, or non-refundable material costs in violation of this policy constitutes a breach of contract. The client agrees to reimburse Copper Sparrows Attic Restoration LLC for any financial penalties, chargeback fees, or legal collection costs incurred as a result of an invalid or fraudulent dispute.

5. Quality Concerns & Warranty Resolution

If you believe an aspect of our completed service did not meet the agreed-upon scope of work, you must notify us in writing within 7 business days of project completion. We reserve the right to inspect the property and perform corrective workmanship in accordance with our Workmanship Warranty before any financial adjustments or chargebacks are considered.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our refund or transaction practices, please contact us at coppersparrowsattic@gmail.com.
