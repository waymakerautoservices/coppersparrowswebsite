Why Us?

The Problem

Most pest companies trap rodents and leave. Most insulation companies blow over contaminated, flattened insulation. Leaving biological hazards, entry points, and crushed R-value behind costs you thousands in heating loss and structural damage.

The Solution

We provide a top-to-bottom restoration. We remove hazards, sanitize the space, permanently seal out wildlife, air-seal the thermal envelope, and upgrade your insulation to modern energy codes—all in one efficient process.

Our Services

  • 01

    Comprehensive Inspection

    Visual and thermal check for insulation depth, pest activity, air leaks, ventilation blockages, and moisture indicators.

  • 02

    Bio-Hazard Cleanout

    HEPA-vacuuming and removal of contaminated, flattened insulation, nesting debris, animal waste, and old storage clutter.

  • 03

    Sanitation & Deodorization

    Application of professional-grade sanitizers and enzymatic agents to eliminate pathogens and rodent pheromone trails.

  • 04

    Wildlife Exclusion

    Permanent sealing of exterior entry points and vents using chew-proof hardware cloth, flashing, and copper mesh.

  • 05

    Thermal Air Sealing

    Sealing all top plates, wire penetrations, and bypasses between the living space and the attic to stop drafts.

  • 06

    Insulation Installation

    Blowing in high-performance cellulose or Rockwool insulation to target R-values, maximizing energy savings.

Non-Toxic & Humane Standards

Safe, Non-Toxic Insulation

We exclusively install materials prioritizing your indoor air quality. Our Blown-In Cellulose uses up to 85% recycled paper treated with natural borates. Our Rockwool is spun from natural volcanic rock and steel slag with zero toxic off-gassing.

Humane & Permanent Exclusion

We believe in permanent physical defense, not lingering chemical hazards. Instead of secondary poisons, we completely seal exterior vulnerabilities using chew-proof hardware cloth and copper mesh—keeping wildlife safely outside.
