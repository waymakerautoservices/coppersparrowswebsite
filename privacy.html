To facilitate our Inspection-First Model, schedule appointments, and provide accurate estimates, we collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:

  • Contact Information: Full name, property address in Connecticut, phone number, and email address.

We use the information we collect strictly for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations under Connecticut law, including the Connecticut Home Improvement Act (HIA).
